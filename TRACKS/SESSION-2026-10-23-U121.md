## Next flash — F-61 bench plan (STA retry heap gate)

The fix for the returning crash is **already in this source tree** and is **not yet flashed**. It is the owner's exact report: *"the heap drops from 109 KB to 15 KB if the Bluetooth is turned on and the Wi-Fi drops after that and the car freezes."*

- **Root cause**: when the STA (router link) drops while BT is live, the rejoin retry loop in `handle()` (`WiFi.disconnect()` + `WiFi.begin()` every `WIFI_RETRY_INTERVAL_MS`, up to `STA_MAX_RETRIES`) churns the radio and eats the last kilobytes until `loop()` locks and the task watchdog resets the car.
- **Gate**: `STA_RETRY_HEAP_FLOOR_BYTES` (== `LINK_ADMISSION_FLOOR_BYTES` == 32000) in `WebServerComm.cpp`, inside the `pendingRejoin_ / !startedAsAP` retry branch (search `sta-retry-heap`). Below the floor the retry is **deferred**, not consumed: neither the retry budget nor the backoff stamp moves, the incumbent link is untouched, and the retry resumes on the first pass the heap is back over the floor (e.g. once BT is turned off). The defer is latched and reported as `[REJOIN] retry deferred: low heap (free=%u floor=%u)` and as `link_cap` on the next `STATE` line.
- **What to flash**: the current `Genum_4WD4M_CAR/` tree as-is. Nothing else on this list changes the crash path.
- **Bench sequence to run**:
  1. flash the car;
  2. turn BT on; confirm the car stays up and `[HEAPW] spp-connected free=...` prints;
  3. join the car's own AP, open the web page, confirm `page_on` is on and `/status` returns `"link_cap":1`;
  4. on the phone, switch the car to a saved router that is **out of range or wrong password** so the STA cannot associate;
  5. confirm the serial shows `[REJOIN] retry deferred: low heap (free=%u floor=%u)` and **no** repeated `[RADIO] retry/disconnect` churn loop;
  6. confirm BT keeps driving (incumbent link untouched) and the car does **not** reset;
  7. turn BT off on the car; confirm the retry resumes and either joins or gives up to AP-only after the budget spends;
  8. confirm `/status` reports `"link_cap":0` while the retry is deferred, and `"crash_count"` did not increment.
- **Pass criterion**: the owner's sequence (BT on → router drop) produces the defer line on serial, the BT link keeps driving, the retry resumes after BT goes away, and the car does not reset. If there is no defer line and the car resets, the binary on the phone is not the one with this gate — re-flash from this tree.
